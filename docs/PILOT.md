# Community pilot and evaluation pack

**Prepared materials only. No participants recruited, consent obtained, feedback collected or outcomes measured.** Real recruitment, account creation, customer contact and email delivery require approval and consent. Use the synthetic local demonstration for rehearsal first.

## Participants and consent

Target 8–15 consenting adults: salon owner, available employees and customers. Record anonymous participant IDs and role; do not invent employee availability or participant counts. Explain purpose, optional participation, data collected, payment arrangements, privacy, withdrawal, support contact and retention. ## Pilot procedure

1. Confirm owner configuration, access permissions, policy wording, backup/recovery, payment reconciliation and notification delivery approval.
2. Rehearse on synthetic data and test browser/mobile devices, slow connections and conflict handling.
3. Brief each participant and obtain consent. Use observed tasks without coaching first; intervene only when needed and record assistance.
4. Customer tasks: register/login, browse service/fee, accept policy, reserve a slot, find reference, manually open WhatsApp (do not send without consent), inspect status, request change/cancel and edit profile.
5. Staff tasks: customer lookup, create a booking, record external payment information, recognise verifier restrictions and manage assigned appointments.
6. Owner tasks: configure service/hours, independently verify/reject payment, inspect pending confirmations, record expense, filter/export reports and inspect notification/audit logs.
7. Reconcile every real pilot receipt against independently observed external money records. The application does not process payment. Never invent money received to complete a task.
8. Ask feedback questions, record issues anonymously, prioritise changes and run two documented iteration cycles. Stop the pilot on unresolved payment/account/privacy or double-booking defects.

## Usability observation checklist

- [ ] Participant understands demonstration versus real business information.
- [ ] Navigation and controls visible at actual phone/desktop size.
- [ ] Can locate service, price, duration and booking fee.
- [ ] Understands Africa/Maseru times and hold expiry.
- [ ] Reads policy and distinguishes request from confirmed booking.
- [ ] Understands WhatsApp requires manual sending and is not payment.
- [ ] Can locate reference, status and assistance.
- [ ] Verifier independently checks money instead of trusting screenshot.
- [ ] Role restrictions are understandable; no other customer's private records accessible.
- [ ] Form errors understandable; loading/network failures recoverable.
- [ ] Can reschedule/cancel within policy and interpret a conflict.
- [ ] Reports distinguish receipts from booked value.
- [ ] Keyboard focus/labels/text contrast usable; note accessibility barriers.

Record task start/end, success/partial/failure, assistance, errors and observation; do not infer satisfaction from silence.

## Feedback questionnaire

Rate 1 strongly disagree through 5 strongly agree; allow “not applicable”.

1. I could find the service, duration and full price.
2. I understood the booking fee and cancellation policy.
3. I understood when my appointment became confirmed.
4. I understood the manual payment and WhatsApp steps.
5. I could find and manage my booking.
6. Errors and status messages helped me recover.
7. The site worked well on my device.
8. I felt confident that private information was protected.
9. I would be comfortable using this system again.
10. Overall, I was satisfied with the experience.

Open questions: What was confusing? What worked well? What is missing? What would you change first? For staff/owner: Were verification and reporting clear? Record exact consented comments anonymously; do not fabricate quotations.
